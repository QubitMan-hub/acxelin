Tunnels whose keys a quantum computer cannot recover.

Acxelin VPN connects your sites over IPsec and your people over WireGuard. Every tunnel's keys are agreed over post-quantum mutual TLS with certificates from your own authority, replaced on a schedule, and refused the moment a site or person is revoked.

Two kinds of tunnel, one way of agreeing keys

Both ends prove who they are with ML-DSA certificates over TLS 1.3 with hybrid ML-KEM, and derive the tunnel's keys from that session. Nothing secret crosses the network, and a peer without a valid certificate gets no keys at all.

One file per person

The administrator invites someone from the console or with acxelin-vpn invite. The invitation holds the enrollment address, the authority's fingerprint and a one-time token, so nobody copies four values by hand.

The person opens it in the desktop app, chooses a passphrase, and connects. The key is made on their computer and never leaves it.

The console, for the administrator

Each site's tunnels with their key exchange, encryption and last rotation; who is connected, since when, their traffic and when their key was last renewed; an access history from each gateway (connections, refusals and why, people cut off) beside the invitations and revocations, each in an owner-only audit log; and health checks that say what to do.

It runs on the administrator's machine and listens there only. Revoking someone takes two clicks, and their tunnel is cut at the gateway's next check.

Where it runs

Gateways

A Docker image with strongSwan 6.1, OpenSSL 3.5 and WireGuard's tools, or systemd units on the host. Each release publishes the image to GHCR.

docker run --network host --cap-add NET_ADMIN -v /etc/acxelin-vpn:/etc/acxelin-vpn ghcr.io/acxelinquantum/acxelin-vpn:{{VERSION}}

People's computers

A Windows Setup, a macOS disk image and a Linux package, each with OpenSSL 3.5 inside. WireGuard is the only other thing to install.

What it does not claim

  • WireGuard's own handshake is classical. The post-quantum protection for people is the pre-shared key mixed into it, replaced every two minutes.
  • Phones, and the IKEv2 clients built into operating systems, cannot do the key agreement, so they are not supported.
  • A revoked site or person keeps access until their gateway has the new revocation list: up to 15 seconds, plus the fetch interval when the list is fetched.
  • On Windows, the kill switch can lift for a moment while the tunnel is recreated.
  • The desktop installers are not code-signed yet.

Connect two sites and a laptop

For remote access alone, acxelin-vpn setup vpn.example.com creates the authority, the gateway's certificate and a checked configuration in one step. The set-up guide goes from the authority to a protected laptop, with the commands for each step. Sites joined by quantum key distribution can mix its keys in (ETSI GS QKD 014).
